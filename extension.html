Flexible reading

  • Automatic scrolling from 0.2× to 5×
  • Manual wheel and scrollbar control during playback
  • Built-in and custom performance cues
  • Adjustable text size, brightness, and eye-line guide

Practical overlay

  • Drag and resize from clearly marked controls
  • Compact floating pill when minimized
  • Continues through supported webpage fullscreen modes
  • Close instantly without changing the underlying page

Private by default

  • No account, analytics, advertising, or backend
  • No browsing-history collection
  • No reading or transmission of webpage content

Connected to the Android app

Need a teleprompter above mobile camera, recording, communication, or presentation apps? The Android version provides a native floating overlay for that workflow.

GET IT ON Google Play

Permission transparency

Access begins when you press Start Prompter.

The extension uses Chrome’s storage permission for your local scripts, plus active-tab and scripting access to place the teleprompter on the webpage you explicitly choose. It does not inspect or transmit the page’s content.
