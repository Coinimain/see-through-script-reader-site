Privacy Policy for See Through Teleprompter

Effective date: September 20, 2026

See Through Teleprompter is a local-first Windows desktop application published by Eric_Dev.

Information handled by the app

The app allows users to enter, edit, save, and display scripts. Scripts, preferences, and teleprompter settings are stored locally on the user's device.

Data collection and transmission

See Through Teleprompter does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or upload scripts or personal information to the developer or any third party. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, or online tracking.

How information is used and stored

Information entered into the app is used only to provide its script-editing and teleprompter functions. This information remains on the user's Windows device and is stored within the app's local storage.

Third-party disclosure

The developer does not receive or disclose user scripts or personal information to third parties. Microsoft may independently process Store installation, licensing, diagnostic, or usage information under Microsoft's own privacy policies.

User controls

Users can edit or delete saved scripts from within the app. Users can also remove locally stored app information by uninstalling the application or managing its app data through Windows.

Security

Because information is stored locally, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their Windows device and account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. Any updated policy will apply from the effective date displayed on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Coinimain GitHub profile or through the support contact provided on the Microsoft Store listing.
