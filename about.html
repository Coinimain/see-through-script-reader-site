About the app

A teleprompter that respects the rest of your screen.

See Through Script Reader began with a straightforward idea: creators should be able to keep their words visible without giving up the recording tools and apps they already prefer.

01

Stay in your workflow

The app is built around a floating overlay rather than forcing every user into a new camera or recording system.

02

Control without clutter

03

Own your scripts

Scripts and settings remain local. Backups are exported only when the user chooses.

Why it exists

Many people record on their phones but keep scripts somewhere else: a notes app, another screen, a printed page, or a document positioned below the camera. That creates unnecessary eye movement, missed points, repeated takes, and more editing.

See Through Script Reader places the script directly where it is needed. The text can scroll automatically, remain stationary, jump to another section, or resume from a saved position while the app underneath remains the user’s choice.

Who it is for

The app is intended for video creators, educators, course instructors, presenters, journalists, founders, sales teams, social-media professionals, and anyone who regularly speaks to a camera or audience from prepared material.

It is equally useful for full scripts and lightweight prompts. Cue markers, line highlighting, reading presets, and manual position controls allow different working styles.

Product principles

Useful before impressive

Every feature should reduce friction during preparation, recording, or repeat takes. Decorative complexity should never get in the way of the script.

Local unless there is a clear reason not to be

The current app does not require an account, cloud synchronization, analytics, or a managed backend. Local storage keeps the product simpler and gives users direct control over their writing.

Power without intimidation

Advanced options belong in Settings; everyday controls belong close to the prompter. The experience should remain approachable on the first day and capable enough for daily professional use.

Development status

See Through Script Reader is currently being prepared for its first public Android release. Features and wording on this website may be refined before launch. The privacy policy will always describe the behavior of the version currently distributed to users.
